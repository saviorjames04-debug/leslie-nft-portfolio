Artist Statement

About Leslie

I create original digital artwork that combines imagination, technology, and visual storytelling. My portfolio is built around individual pieces that can stand alone as art while also becoming part of larger collectible series.

I am developing my work for online exhibition, digital collecting, licensing, and NFT publication. Each listed piece should include its title, description, edition size, creation date, and blockchain link after it is minted.

Publishing Checklist

Before You Publish

  1. Replace the six sample tiles with your real artwork files.
  2. Add a unique title and description for every piece.
  3. Keep your original high-resolution files backed up.
  4. Create your OpenSea account and collection.
  5. Mint only artwork you own or have permission to use.
  6. Add each OpenSea item link back to this portfolio.
